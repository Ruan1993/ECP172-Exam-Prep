## Source hierarchy and limits

They take precedence over the older FIS study plan, which still anticipated that briefing and described Q4 differently. This audit does not claim access to an independent recording, official marking memorandum, or any later lecturer clarification. The focused notes are available as a local text extraction; the original focused-notes PDF was not found.

## Discrepancies corrected

- Q1: abbreviated legislation/policy names were expanded to the supplied titles and Act numbers. Purposes matched the course sources. BELA remains scenario-dependent; no new provisions were invented.
- Q2: the condensed proposal framework omitted secondary questions and the explicit findings/recommendations distinction from final guidance. Added that chain alongside the clearly labelled worked-example framework, aligned bullying questions/objectives, ethical clearance, anonymity/confidentiality, justified instruments, significance, limitations, withdrawal and the complete guide's explanatory sequential design. These are examples, not a prescribed design for every cyberbullying issue.
- Q3: added listening to LAC; filled verified gaps concerning language challenges, teacher development/resources, comprehensible input, balanced skills, medical/social models and four differentiation areas. LoLT, LiEP and SIAS remain distinct.
- Q4: knowledge construction now explicitly includes collaborative learning as emphasised in the focused notes, alongside the guide's perspective-comparison explanation. Added classroom diversity, the five cultural-interaction areas and all stated goals, including empowerment. The same four approach names are retained.
